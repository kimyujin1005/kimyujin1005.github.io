Profile

Yujin Kim

Ph.D. Student
Computer Architecture Researcher

I am a Ph.D. student in Engineering with an emphasis in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of California, Merced. I work with Prof. Hyeran Jeon in the MoCA (Merced Computer Architecture) Lab.

My research interests include computer architecture and AI accelerator architecture. I am particularly interested in efficient numerical representations, neural-network accelerators, memory systems for AI workloads, and hardware–software co-design.

Before joining UC Merced, I earned an M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Korea University and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Soongsil University. My earlier work includes RISC-V and SoC design, cycle-accurate simulation, and efficient accelerators for deep-learning workloads.
